# Self Onboarding

This guide is for a non-technical owner setting up Flow OS without engineering help.

## Steps

1. Create an account
2. Choose the business profile
3. Fill in business name, phone, email, and address
4. Add opening hours and branch locations
5. Add team members
6. Connect calendars and telephony
7. Set AI tone and emergency wording
8. Upload policies, pricing, and FAQs
9. Test the receptionist
10. Review readiness and launch

## What the owner should see

- Clear status labels
- Missing setup items
- Safe test scenarios
- A single go-live score
