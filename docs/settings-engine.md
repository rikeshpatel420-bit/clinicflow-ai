# Settings Engine

The settings engine stores the reusable operational rules for a business.

## What it covers

- SMS templates
- email templates
- dashboard wording
- CRM fields
- workflow stages
- follow-up cadence
- escalation rules
- notification tone

## Why it exists

It keeps operational copy out of the core platform and lets each business customise wording without rewriting the app.

## Relationship to other modules

- Branding engine controls the visual identity
- Prompt studio controls the receptionist voice
- Knowledge base controls the facts and rules
- Settings engine controls the operational defaults

## Good practice

- Keep wording short and clear
- Avoid mixing operational labels with UI labels
- Reuse the same templates across voice, SMS, and dashboard copy
